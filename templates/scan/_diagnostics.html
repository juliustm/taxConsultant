{# Self-service troubleshooting. A field user with a broken app and no admin nearby has otherwise no way to tell "my phone refused the camera" from "the install never finished" from "there is no internet". Every check here names one thing and, when it fails, says what to do - and the repair button covers the single most common cause, a precache that a weak connection left half-filled. #} {# run() is deliberately not the initialiser any more. It compiles the ZXing WASM, re-fetches the worker source, walks the whole precache and queries permissions - none of which should happen because someone opened the app to scan something. It runs when this view is first opened, and again on every later visit, which is what a diagnostics screen is for. #}
