How retries work

Attempts are spaced further apart each time, and the spacing depends on what went wrong: a receipt the vendor has not uploaded is worth waiting a day for, while a rate-limited request is worth retrying in minutes. Nothing is retried inside the request that discovered the failure — the next attempt is written on the job and picked up by a later run.
